1. Game Day!
Leon couldn't wait to watch his beloved Bombers! Mum, Dad, Eden and Ruby were just as excited. Little did they know, today would be extraordinary.
2. Trouble at the MCG
Suddenly, a message flashed across the giant screen. The Essendon team bus had broken down! Five players were missing. How could the Bombers possibly win?
3. The Chosen Five
The Essendon coach spotted Leon and his family in the crowd. “You five! We need your help!” Leon couldn't believe it. His biggest dream was coming true!
4. Here Come the Bombers!
Wearing their official Essendon guernseys, the family raced onto the mighty MCG. Thousands of fans cheered as Leon led his family onto the field.
5. Eden Takes Flight
The umpire threw the ball high into the air. Eden leapt above her opponent and tapped it straight towards Mum. What an incredible start!
6. Ruby's Mighty Tackle
A Cats player grabbed the footy, but Ruby charged towards him. She wrapped him up in a mighty tackle. “BALL!” roared the excited crowd.
7. Dad to the Rescue
Leon scooped up the footy and raced away. A huge defender charged towards him, but Dad delivered a brilliant shepherd. “Keep running, Leon!” he shouted.
8. Mum's Super Kick
Leon handballed to Mum, who launched an enormous kick downfield. A Cats player dived desperately to smother it, but the footy sailed straight past him!
9. Leon's Spectacular Specky
Leon sprinted towards the falling ball, leapt onto a defender's shoulders and soared into the sky. He grabbed the footy! “WHAT A SPECKY!” screamed the commentator.
10. The Winning Goal
Five points behind, with seconds remaining, Leon lined up his kick. The footy sailed between the big sticks. The siren sounded. THE BOMBERS HAD WON!
11. A Family of Superstars
Fans flooded the field, lifting Leon and his family onto their shoulders. The entire stadium celebrated their new heroes. It was the greatest footy day ever!
